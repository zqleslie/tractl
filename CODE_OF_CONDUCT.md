# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as contributors and maintainers pledge to make participation in traCtl a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity and expression, level of experience, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our Standards

**Positive behaviours:**
- Using welcoming and inclusive language
- Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
- Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
- Focusing on what is best for the community
- Showing empathy towards other community members

**Unacceptable behaviours:**
- Trolling, insulting/derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
- Public or private harassment
- Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
- Sexualised language or imagery
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Our Responsibilities

Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying the standards of acceptable behaviour and are expected to take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any instances of unacceptable behaviour.

Maintainers have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments, commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned to this Code of Conduct.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies in all project spaces — GitHub issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other official traCtl communication channels.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behaviour may be reported by opening a **private** GitHub issue labelled `conduct` or by emailing the maintainers directly (see README for contact).

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are obligated to maintain confidentiality with regard to the reporter of an incident.

## Enforcement Guidelines

| Violation | Consequence |
|-----------|-------------|
| Minor inappropriate language or behaviour | Private written warning with clarity around the nature of the violation |
| Single serious violation or repeated minor violations | Temporary ban from interaction in project spaces |
| Sustained inappropriate behaviour or harassment | Permanent ban |
